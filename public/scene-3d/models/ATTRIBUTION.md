## Party guests: Microsoft Rocketbox

Business_Male_03 and Business_Female_01, using the library's authored seated animation. Original character meshes, UVs and painted textures are preserved. The seated pose is baked for efficient rendering; materials are converted to glTF and textures compressed for the web.

Source: [Microsoft Rocketbox](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), [MIT license](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/blob/master/LICENSE.md).

The current night party uses the original Business_Male_03 and Business_Female_01 rigs with the library's authored m_cheer_01, f_cheer_01 and f_claphands_01 clips. Two passes of curved subdivision refine the geometry while interpolating the original UVs and skin weights. Original 2K clothing/face textures and head/body normal maps are retained; selected excerpts drive cheering and clapping with scroll. The seated versions are archived assets and are no longer requested by the hero.
